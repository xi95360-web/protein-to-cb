# Interpreting the user's results

`raw_stateN` is a sum of conditional natural-log probabilities and is normally negative. `pmpnn_stateN` is mutant raw minus its separately scored WT baseline on that same backbone; a negative delta means lower model sequence/backbone compatibility. Neither sign directly reports activity or thermodynamic stability. Different backbone coverage makes cross-state raw totals unsuitable for direct comparison.

If both deltas are negative, a less-negative delta in one state provides a conditional descriptive preference signal. Do not call it proof of a conformational population shift. Native CB standardizes the two columns over the supplied panel before taking their difference: WT plus one mutant with same-direction deltas becomes identical ±1 in each column and a zero bias. State this degeneracy explicitly, prioritize raw/WT-relative values, and do not report the zero as biological neutrality. Do not enlarge the panel solely to force a result; a scientific background panel must be justified and authorized.

CB ranks sequences against fixed reference states. It does not generate mutant coordinates, estimate state occupancy, compute free energies or predict arbitrary functions. When a particular state governs a function, biased candidates can be enriched for a corresponding functional change. Evidence must be target-specific. The original study retrospectively compared state-related functional readouts and prospectively tested LplA with SAXS/Trp fluorescence and protein-labeling assays (Science DOI 10.1126/science.adv7953, Fig. 2–5). LplA's prospective selection also required at least one state score above WT; this is a study-specific selection choice, not a universal rule.

For STING G166E, distinguish the current fixed-backbone single-chain calculation from established gain-of-function assays. König et al. reported increased ligand-independent IRF3/IFN-β signaling (DOI 10.1136/annrheumdis-2016-209841, Fig. 2). Their interface mechanism was proposed using modeling/docking. Single-chain scoring omits the partner-chain contacts and cannot establish or overturn that mechanism. Increased basal signaling is also distinct from a lower ligand EC50 or a lower physical activation barrier.
